🔒 Privacy & Security

✓ Your Data, Your Device

All calculations and measurements are processed locally on your device. No data is sent to external servers. No tracking, no ads, no unwanted permissions.

PlotCalc is an open-source project for complete transparency. Review the source code anytime on GitHub.
